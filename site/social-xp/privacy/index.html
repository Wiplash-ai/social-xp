Privacy policy

Local means local.

Effective August 1, 2026

What Social-XP stores

Social-XP stores the social site, activity type, timestamp, XP and reward history, goals, theme preference, widget position, and a non-readable deduplication fingerprint in your browser's local extension storage. It does not store the text of your posts or replies.

What Social-XP transmits

Social-XP V1 does not transmit your browsing history, post content, social activity, extension settings, or identifiers to Wiplash.ai or another backend service.

Possible future sync service

A future version may offer an optional Social-XP account and API service that synchronizes progress, levels, XP, goals, and preferences across multiple browsers. Before introducing that service, we will update this policy, explain what data is transmitted and retained, and ask users to opt in. The current version does not provide or use this service.

Site access

The extension runs on supported social sites only to detect user-initiated post and reply flows and to render the optional Social-XP widget. This access is not used to build a remote browsing profile.

Control

You can correct or clear tracked activity from the extension interface. Removing the extension also removes its local browser storage according to your browser's extension-data behavior.

Contact

Questions can be sent to support@wiplash.ai.
